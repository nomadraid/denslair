// about this lair

Den's Lair is Denis' cozy corner of the internet — part portfolio, part living room.

The TV plays films that are legally free to watch: public-domain classics embedded from the Internet Archive, plus free-with-ads titles embedded straight from YouTube. Nothing is hosted or copied here — if a tape refuses to play (age-restricted ones do), the TV will hand you a link to watch it at the source.

The room itself is hand-drawn SVG + CSS animation + a sprinkle of vanilla JS. No frameworks, no build step, no tracking. Deployed on the edge.
